READ THE MODEL

Local decisions. An explicit safety layer.

01

Private knowledge

Robots plan through their own discovered map. Gossip exchanges snapshots only with visible neighbors in range. Sensors and neighbor poses are idealized.

02

Central simulation physics

A central shield checks full moves and simultaneous robot paths. It holds conflicts with rotating priority. This is not a decentralized collision controller.

03

Measured limits

Coverage uses free cell centres reachable from the starts. Blocked proposals and long no-motion stalls are reported. No hardware or optimality claim is made.

03 / COMPARE

90 runs. Three different spaces.

Seeds 1–10, six robots, the same start positions per seed, a 400-tick maximum and a 95% visited target. All policies use the same sensor and safety rules. Each run stops when it reaches the target or exhausts its limit.

Error bars show sample standard deviation, not confidence intervals. These fixed synthetic maps are a small experiment; performance may change on other maps or settings. Earlier stopping at the target limits terminal coverage.
